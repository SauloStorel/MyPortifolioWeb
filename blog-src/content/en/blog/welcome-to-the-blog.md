Hi, I'm Saulo.

## Who I am

I'm 20 and I live in Teresina, in Piauí, Brazil. I've liked programming since I was a kid, and the biggest influence was my uncle, who is also a programmer.

Right now I'm in my first job, which is actually an internship, at the FMS in Teresina (the city's Municipal Health Foundation). I work in the HR department as a systems developer, and I take care of the whole HR system of the FMS and its branches.

Outside the internship I run Storell, which is my work building websites and systems, almost always with Ruby on Rails.

## What I've built

- [DeMolay Piauí](/en/projects/demolay-piaui/): the Order's website, with a registration store, InfinitePay payments, a transparency portal, check-in and event certificates.
- [CRN Cap](/en/projects/crn-cap/): the system for the Order's CRN and CNIE campaigns, with five access profiles and a ranking.
- [Xfria](/en/projects/xfria/): an ESP32 that imitates the air conditioner's remote control, so you can use it from your computer and phone.
- [rhut](/en/projects/rhut/): screen sharing for Discord straight from the browser.
