Welcome to the Storell blog

I'm 20, from Teresina, Brazil, and I've loved programming since I was a kid. This blog is where I write about my projects: ideas, challenges, how and with what.

Hi, I’m Saulo.

Who I am#

I’m 20 and I live in Teresina, in Piauí, Brazil. I’ve liked programming since I was a kid, and the biggest influence was my uncle, who is also a programmer.

Right now I’m in my first job, which is actually an internship, at the FMS in Teresina (the city’s Municipal Health Foundation). I work in the HR department as a systems developer, and I take care of the whole HR system of the FMS and its branches.

Outside the internship I run Storell, which is my work building websites and systems, almost always with Ruby on Rails.

What I’ve built#

  • DeMolay Piauí: the Order’s website, with a registration store, InfinitePay payments, a transparency portal, check-in and event certificates.
  • CRN Cap: the system for the Order’s CRN and CNIE campaigns, with five access profiles and a ranking.
  • Xfria: an ESP32 that imitates the air conditioner’s remote control, so you can use it from your computer and phone.
  • rhut: screen sharing for Discord straight from the browser.

Talk to me#
